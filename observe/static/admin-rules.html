Threshold rules

Rules are evaluated

Rules are validated and stored, and every change is audited. A saved rule applies at the next poll of a monitor or the next pushed batch. A rule that holds Warning or Critical raises the host to Warn or Down through the usual confirmation counts, so alerts follow the normal path. A window rule is judged only when the last 100 samples reach back over its window.
